# Security policy

Please do not report security vulnerabilities in public issues or discussions.

Email [support@inttegro.com](mailto:support@inttegro.com) with the subject `Security report` and include:

- A clear description of the issue and its likely impact.
- Reproduction steps or a minimal proof of concept.
- The affected plugin, skill, package, endpoint, or version.
- Any suggested remediation or relevant disclosure timeline.

Do not access or modify customer data while investigating. We will coordinate validation, remediation, and responsible disclosure directly with the reporter.

For ordinary product support, use [support@inttegro.com](mailto:support@inttegro.com).
