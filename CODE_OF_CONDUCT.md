# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place for thoughtful, practical collaboration.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Welcome different backgrounds, experience levels, and points of view.
- Critique ideas and implementations without attacking people.
- Respect privacy and never expose customer, contributor, or security-sensitive information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and help repair harm.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project publicly.

Report conduct concerns privately to [support@inttegro.com](mailto:support@inttegro.com). Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
